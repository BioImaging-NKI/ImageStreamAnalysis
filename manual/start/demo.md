# Try it in your browser

The live demo is the dashboard of the example dataset *Donor 1* (synthetic data; see
[Your first analysis](first-analysis.md)), exactly as the program writes it:

**[Open the live demo](https://bioimaging-nki.github.io/ImageStreamAnalysis/demo/dashboard.html){ target="_blank" }**

Everything works as it does with your own results. You can hover over and click cells, drag the subtype thresholds,
click a square of the interaction matrix, and change the membrane pair. The page runs entirely in your browser, and
nothing is sent anywhere. [The dashboard](../guide/dashboard.md) describes every part of the page.
