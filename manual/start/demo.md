# Try it in your browser

The dashboard of the example dataset *Donor 1* (synthetic data, see [Your first analysis](first-analysis.md)), as
the program writes it:

**[Open the live demo](https://bioimaging-nki.github.io/ImageStreamAnalysis/demo/dashboard.html){ target="_blank" }**

Everything works as with your own results: hover and click cells, drag the subtype thresholds, click a square of the
interaction matrix, and change the membrane pair. Nothing is sent anywhere; the page runs in your browser.
[The dashboard](../guide/dashboard.md) describes every part.
